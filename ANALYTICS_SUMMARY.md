## What Psychometric Indices Mean

### Difficulty Index (0.0 - 1.0)
**Formula:** Students who got it right ÷ Total students

- **0.70+** = 🟢 EASY (too easy, can't measure differences)
- **0.30-0.69** = 🟡 MODERATE (ideal range)
- **Below 0.30** = 🔴 DIFFICULT (too hard, might be flawed)

### Discrimination Index (-1.0 to 1.0)
**Formula:** (Top 27% correct) - (Bottom 27% correct)

- **0.40+** = ✅ EXCELLENT (separates high/low performers)
- **0.30-0.39** = 👍 GOOD (adequate separation)
- **0.20-0.29** = ⚠️ FAIR (weak separation)
- **Below 0.20** = ❌ POOR (doesn't separate or negative)

**Negative discrimination = Red flag!** Low performers did BETTER than high performers = question is broken
